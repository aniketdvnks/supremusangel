1. The three schemes

Sales Person

SA Incentive Calculation

Individual seller. Incentive = % of salary + reward on sales above target.

Team Lead

SA TL Incentive Calculation

Personal (bonus + marginal incentive) + flat 1% team commission.

Branch Manager

SA BM Incentive Calculation

Same personal half + tiered 0.5% / 1% branch commission.

All share a monthly window, base target = 10 × salary, minimum = 7 × salary, and confirmed SA Sales Records as the sales source.

2. Hierarchy & calculation order

Teams and branches are the ERPNext Sales Person tree (nested set). A manager's members are the non-group (leaf) Sales Persons in its subtree; group nodes are organisational and excluded.

All Sales Persons (group)
└─ Ravi (BM)            group
   ├─ Anil (TL)         group
   │  ├─ Sunil          leaf  ← seller
   │  └─ Sneha          leaf  ← seller
   └─ Priya (TL)        group
      ├─ Raj            leaf  ← seller
      └─ Maya           leaf  ← seller
Managers read each member's salary/target from that member's own SA Incentive Calculation, and read member sales from SA Sales Record. Always calculate sellers → TLs → BM, in that order.

3. Sales Person logic

base_target      = salary × 10
total_sales      = Σ Confirmed SA Sales Record (month)
achievement %    = total_sales / base_target × 100
incentive_amount = salary × slab.incentive_percent / 100
reward_amount    = (total_sales − base_target) × slab.reward_percent / 100   [if > 100%]
total_payout     = incentive_amount + reward_amount

Personal half (bonus % of target + marginal incentive)

Marginal = each band's rate applies only to the portion of sales inside that band.

Team half

full_team_target = Σ (member salary × 10)
team_target      = 70% × full_team_target
team_sales       = Σ member confirmed sales
team_achievement = team_sales / full_team_target × 100
team_commission  = 1% × team_sales        [only if team_achievement ≥ 70%]
total_payout     = personal_payout + team_commission

5. Branch Manager logic

Personal half is identical to TL (reuses the same bonus/incentive slabs). The only difference is the tiered branch commission:

Branch achievement (vs full branch target)Commission
Below 70%0
70–100% (on target)0.5% of branch revenue
Above 100% (overachieved)1% of branch revenue
total_payout = personal_payout + branch_commission

12. Verification checklist

  • Below 70% personal → no personal bonus/incentive (all schemes).
  • TL/BM personal bonus is % of target (not salary): 3% × 5,00,000 = 15,000 for a TL at 100%.
  • TL/BM personal incentive is marginal (6,00,000 on 5,00,000 → 10,000, not 60,000).
  • TL team commission is flat 1% once team ≥ 70%.
  • BM branch commission: 0 below 70%, 0.5% at 70–100%, 1% above 100%.
  • Member list = leaf sellers in subtree (group nodes excluded).
  • Member without a monthly SA Incentive Calculation → orange warning; sales counted, target excluded.
  • Re-running Calculate is idempotent.
